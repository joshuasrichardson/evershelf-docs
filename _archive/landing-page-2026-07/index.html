Frequently asked questions

A straightforward, judgment-free visit.

Do I need existing food storage?
No. We can audit what you currently have—even if it is only one pantry shelf—and help you build a practical plan from there.
Will you throw away expired food?
Nothing is discarded without your permission. We identify dates and concerns, then let you make the final decision using manufacturer guidance and your own judgment.
Can you shop for me?
Yes. Our Concierge service can shop for recommended items, coordinate delivery, and put supplies away. You approve every purchase before anything is bought.
Do you sell food?
No. EverShelf provides inventory, organization, and planning services. When requested, we purchase approved items from retailers on your behalf.
How long does an audit take?
Timing depends on the amount and number of storage areas. We will discuss the scope and estimated visit length before confirming your appointment.
Do I need to clean first?
Not at all. Please leave the space as you normally use it. Seeing the current setup helps us recommend a system that works in real life.
Is this only for large food storage rooms?
No. EverShelf works for pantry cabinets, garages, basements, closets, storage rooms, and households using several smaller areas.
Do you guarantee preparedness?
No. EverShelf provides organizational and preparedness-planning guidance. Inventories, food-security calculations, and recommendations are informational tools—not guarantees.
What areas do you serve?
We currently serve {{ $cfg.service_area }}. Contact us if you are nearby and we will let you know whether your address is within the service area.
Will you take photos?
Only with your permission and only when helpful for documenting inventory or organization. We will explain how any photos will be used before taking them.
