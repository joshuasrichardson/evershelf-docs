Consumer Health Data Privacy Notice

Effective July 31, 2026

EverShelf’s public preparedness planner includes optional questions that may relate to food avoidances, accessibility, pregnancy, infant feeding, refrigerated supplies, or power-dependent equipment. You may skip every question.

What we collect

We process only the planner answers you choose to provide. The food-avoidance field asks for foods or food types, not the reason you avoid them.

Why we process it

  • To generate the preparedness plan you request.
  • If you separately opt into product research, to understand de-identified customer needs and improve EverShelf.

What we retain

Plan generation is stateless. Raw answers are not linked to your waitlist contact. With optional research consent, a separate record retains normalized responses for no more than 90 days. It has no name, email, IP address, advertising identifier, or link to your waitlist record. Raw food-avoidance text is not retained; only broad categories and a count may be used for research.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell consumer health data. We do not share planner answers for advertising. Hosting and email providers process limited information on our behalf under service-provider arrangements; the email provider receives waitlist contact information, not research responses.

Your choices

You may skip questions or decline research. If you consent, your confirmation email includes a private one-click link that deletes that de-identified research response. You may request access to or deletion of identifiable waitlist data by contacting support@joshumi.com. Withdrawing research consent does not affect your waitlist status or access to the free plan.
