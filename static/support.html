Subscriptions and purchases

To restore a previous App Store purchase, open Profile in EverShelf and tap Restore Purchases. To manage or cancel an auto-renewable subscription, use Apple’s subscription settings. Deleting an EverShelf account does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Delete your account

In EverShelf, open Profile, select the Profile tab, and tap Delete Account. Confirming permanently deletes the account and its associated EverShelf data, including uploaded media, inventory, plans, community connections, and chat messages.

Report or block a community user

Open the conversation, tap the options button in the upper-right corner, then choose Report user or Block user. Blocking removes the connection and prevents either person from finding or contacting the other in EverShelf.
